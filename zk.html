The key is checked against the curve first, then bound into the proof as a public signal, so the credential proves exactly which key the plaintext was escrowed to and that key cannot be swapped after the fact — the holder opens the credential with scripts/zk-decrypt-escrow.mjs credential.json <private scalar>.
